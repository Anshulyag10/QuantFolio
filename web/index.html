Research

Evidence, not promises.

Every rule is tested out of sample, after costs, with corrections for testing many ideas at once. Figures below are read from the saved experiment outputs.

Method

One pipeline, six steps.

01DataAdjusted prices from Yahoo Finance, with a bundled 94-asset snapshot for offline, reproducible work.
02EstimateBayes-Stein expected returns; Ledoit-Wolf, EWMA or sample covariance.
03OptimiseConvex programs solved exactly under long-only bounds and a per-asset cap.
04MeasureEuler risk contributions, VaR and CVaR under historical, Gaussian and Cornish-Fisher models.
05ValidateWalk-forward backtests, Holm-corrected tests, block-bootstrap intervals and the Deflated Sharpe Ratio.
06SimulateMonte Carlo that follows the backtest's own drift, rebalancing and cost process.
